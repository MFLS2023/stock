# Cross-Source Synthesizer

## Workflow

1. Require at least two integrated source scopes. State which sources are available and which are pending.
2. Search each source separately before combining results. Expand queries with known synonyms: 竞价/竟价/集合竞价, 弱转强/转势, 龙头/核心票/人气核心, 情绪周期/情绪冰点/退潮.
3. Normalize terms only after checking their source-specific definitions. Similar words do not automatically mean identical concepts.
4. Build a comparison with:
   - shared observations;
   - explicit disagreements;
   - different market-regime assumptions;
   - evidence strength and historical dates;
   - practical tests that could distinguish the views.
5. Preserve minority or contradictory views. Do not average them into a single slogan.
6. Attach a traceable citation to every material claim.
7. Save durable synthesis only under `_知识库系统/cross_source/` and keep raw-source text unchanged.

When current applicability is part of the question, invoke `$market-evidence-verifier` and separate live evidence from historical source views.
