# Trading Journal Reviewer

Keep personal records under `_知识库系统/personal/trading_journal/` and separate them from imported blogger materials.

## Workflow

1. Define the review period and available journal fields.
2. Calculate quantitative metrics with code: trade count, win rate, payoff ratio, maximum drawdown when data permits, mode-external rate, and plan-adherence rate.
3. Compare planned triggers, actual entries, planned exits, actual exits, and position size.
4. Group errors by observable behavior rather than moral judgment: impulsive entry, plan deviation, oversized position, delayed exit, revenge trade, missing review, or unclear setup.
5. Use corpus methods only as cited comparison material, never as proof that the user's trade should have worked.
6. Produce:
   - evidence-backed findings;
   - highest-impact repeated pattern;
   - one to three measurable changes for the next period;
   - a follow-up checklist.
7. Protect privacy and do not copy journal data into source libraries or cross-source claims.

Do not provide guaranteed strategies or automatic execution instructions.
